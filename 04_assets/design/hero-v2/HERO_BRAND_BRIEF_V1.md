# SkinIntel Hero Brand Brief V1

## Status

Locked draft for Hero Asset V2 generation.

This document defines the visual direction for the long-term SkinIntel landing hero image.

The landing layout is already locked.  
This brief is for the image only.

---

## Goal

Create a hero image that makes SkinIntel feel like a premium AI skincare companion.

The image must communicate:

- calm trust
- real skin
- personal skin journey
- private AI analysis
- premium wellness
- not medical
- not cosmetic advertising

---

## Core Concept

A centered portrait of a woman with healthy but imperfect real skin, photographed in warm natural morning light, designed as a clean UI canvas for SkinIntel overlays.

The image should feel like:

> “This app helps me understand my skin.”

Not:

> “This is a beauty cream advertisement.”

---

## Subject

- Woman aged 25–40
- Calm, natural, trustworthy expression
- Soft eye contact or slightly off-camera look
- Head and shoulders visible
- Face centered
- Both eyes visible
- Mouth visible
- No dramatic posing
- No fashion/editorial feeling
- No influencer/glam look

---

## Skin Direction

The skin must be realistic.

Required:

- visible natural texture
- slight redness
- subtle uneven tone
- small normal imperfections
- healthy but human skin

Avoid:

- perfect glass skin
- airbrushed skin
- porcelain skin
- heavy makeup
- dramatic acne
- clinical dermatology imagery
- scary medical problem skin

Important phrase:

**Healthy but imperfect skin.**

---

## Lighting

- warm natural morning light
- soft window light
- premium wellness feeling
- warm ivory / beige environment
- gentle shadows
- no harsh flash
- no ring light
- no clinical lighting
- no blue/cold tones

---

## Background

- minimal warm ivory or beige background
- no products
- no bottles
- no mirror
- no bathroom details
- no text
- no logos
- no UI baked into the image

The background must be calm and clean enough for overlay cards.

---

## Composition

The image must work in the existing hero frame:

- mobile portrait crop
- 4:5 and 3:4 compatible
- face centered horizontally
- enough space above hairline
- enough space below chin
- enough clean space top-left for the 92% overlay card
- enough clean space bottom-right for the 14 days overlay card
- AI scan overlay should sit naturally over the mid-face / cheek area
- no important facial feature covered by overlays

---

## Existing UI Overlays

The image will be used behind existing HTML overlays:

- “Pouzdanost analize” / “92%”
- “Praćenje promjena” / “14 dana”
- AI scan glow
- bottom feature strip:
  - Brzo
  - Privatno
  - Praćenje

Do not include any of this text in the image.

---

## What The Image Must NOT Be

Reject if it looks like:

- stock skincare photo
- beauty campaign
- serum advertisement
- influencer selfie
- fashion editorial
- medical diagnosis app
- dermatology clinic
- AI plastic skin
- perfect symmetrical synthetic face
- split-screen before/after
- product bottle ad

---

perfect skin, glass skin, airbrushed, porcelain, plastic skin, heavy makeup, glossy lips, ring light, stock photo, wide smile, cosmetic advertisement, serum ad, product bottle, clinical, dermatology, doctor, gloves, medical, rash, severe acne, split screen, before after, text, logo, watermark, fashion editorial, dramatic shadows, cool blue tones, cut off face, half face, face out of frame, cropped forehead, cropped chin, uncanny AI face, exaggerated symmetry

---

## Approval Checklist

Accept only if:

- full face is visible
- face is centered
- skin looks real
- slight imperfections are visible
- image feels premium and calm
- background is clean
- overlay zones are usable
- no text is baked into the image
- it does not look like a stock beauty ad
- it works as a long-term SkinIntel brand hero

---

## Final Decision Rule

If the image is only “pretty”, reject it.

It must feel like SkinIntel.
